Anti-Social Design

No history. New status overwrites old. The past doesn't matter — only the present. No timestamps (or only vague ones like "just now" or "long ago"). No precise privacy exposure. Resets at midnight.

Invite-Only Connection

No registration needed. Connect through invitation links. When you send a link, you're saying: "I saved a spot for you in my Top 10." That honor makes it worth it, even if it's paid.
