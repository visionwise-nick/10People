2. Service Description

10People is a minimalist "safety check" and "mind connection" application that provides:

  • The 10 Rings: Connect with up to 10 people who matter most in your life
  • Daily Light Up: One-tap status updates that reset at midnight
  • Widget 2.0 (The Window): Home screen widget showing your circle's status
  • The Nudge: Simple notification to check in with your connections
  • Ephemeral Status: No history, no timestamps, anti-social by design
  • Invite-Only Connections: Gift "tickets" to your inner circle

Our Philosophy: "The world is loud, I just want to hear your heartbeat." We deliberately remove social media clutter to create meaningful connections.
