Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026 — Your connections, your privacy

1. Introduction

Welcome to 10People! We care deeply about your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data while you stay connected with your closest 10.

By using 10People, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Device Information

To ensure the app works properly, we collect:

  • Device model and OS version
  • App version
  • A unique device identifier

2.2 Usage Data

We collect anonymized usage data to improve the app:

  • How often you light up
  • Feature usage patterns
  • Crash reports

2.3 Circle Information

Important: To connect you with your circle, we store your display name and your connections' information. This data is used solely for the app's core functionality.

2.4 Activity Records

We keep track of your light-up times to show your status to your circle.

3. Information We DO NOT Collect

Our Promise:
  • We DO NOT track your real-time location.
  • We DO NOT access your contacts without permission.
  • We DO NOT sell your data to third parties.
  • We DO NOT show you ads.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Core Service: To enable the light-up and nudge features.
  • Connections: To show your status to your circle.
  • Improvements: To make the app better for everyone.

5. Data Security

5.1 Storage

Your data is encrypted and stored securely in the cloud.

5.2 Retention

We retain your data until you delete your account. You can request data deletion at any time.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your data.
  • Correction: Update your information.
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data.
